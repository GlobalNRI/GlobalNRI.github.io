Cost of Living in the Netherlands

Estimated Monthly Living Costs

Single Person: Approx. €2,100–€3,300

Family (2 adults + 2 children): Approx. €3,600–€6,000

Actual costs depend on lifestyle, city, housing choices, and energy prices.

Note for families: If your children attend daycare, this can be a major additional cost in the Netherlands. For more information on Kinder daycare and expenses, visit our Daycare and Childcare guide.

Housing

  • 1‑bedroom apartment: €1,000–€1,600/month, Amsterdam and Utrecht at the high end
  • 3‑bedroom apartment: €1,800–€3,200/month in major cities; cheaper in smaller towns

Room Sharing Option: Sharing a room can significantly reduce costs, typically around €1,000/month depending on the city and location. Prices are higher near city centers. Many Indian Facebook groups post room-sharing opportunities—contact them to find options, but always verify the person's identity to avoid fraud, which can occur in the Netherlands.

Utilities & Internet

  • Electricity, water, gas: €170–€270/month depending on usage and weather
  • Internet & phone bundle: €35–€70/month

Food & Groceries

Grocery prices in the Netherlands have increased significantly over the last few years. Indian groceries are generally more expensive compared to regular Dutch supermarkets.

You can read our guide on how to save money while shopping for groceries in the Netherlands.

  • Groceries: €290–€380 per person/month
  • Restaurant meal: €17–€33 per person (higher in Indian restaurants)

Indian restaurants are usually more expensive. A single meal typically costs €22–€33 per person.

Read more: How to Save Money on Groceries in the Netherlands

Healthcare Insurance

  • Health insurance: €150 per adult/month (basic package)
  • For families: €150 for one adult; kids up to 18 are covered free under parents' insurance.

Read more: More information on Health Insurance in the Netherlands

Transportation

  • Public transport subscription: €90–€120/month (varies by region)
  • Biking is common and inexpensive
  • Fuel for cars: €2.10–€2.50/litre

Miscellaneous & Leisure

  • Leisure, dining out, subscriptions: €100–€220/month
  • Entertainment (cinema, events): €10–€25 per visit
