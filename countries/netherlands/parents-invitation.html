Parents' Netherlands Visit: Document Checklist

Prepare the documents below for a short-stay family visit. The table shows what to bring, who prepares it, where to obtain it and whether to submit an original or copy.

Important: Follow the latest NetherlandsWorldwide and VFS checklist for the applicant's location. Requirements can vary by case. Take originals only where requested, keep copies separate and avoid buying non-refundable tickets before the visa decision.

Before the appointment

  • Keep originals and photocopies in separate sections and arrange papers in the order required by VFS.
  • Check that names, passport numbers, travel dates and addresses match across the form, invitation, insurance and itinerary.
  • Confirm translation or legalisation requirements for documents not in an accepted language.
  • Relationship evidence, accommodation details or other supporting documents may be requested for your case even if they are not listed above.

A visit of up to 90 days normally follows the Schengen short-stay process. Longer stays use a different residence-permit route.
