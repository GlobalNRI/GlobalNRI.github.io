Renting a Home in Australia for NRIs

Australian rentals are commonly advertised by weekly price, and rules differ by state or territory. Short-term accommodation can give you time to inspect suburbs before committing to a lease.

Before signing

  • Confirm the weekly rent, lease term, bond, utilities and available move-in date.
  • Attend an inspection or use a trusted representative and verify the listing.
  • Prepare identity, income, references and visa documents for the application.
  • Complete the condition report carefully and return it by the required deadline.

Plan your move-in budget

CostWhat to check
RentListings generally quote weekly rent; calculate the annual cost before applying.
BondPay only through the approved state or territory process where applicable.
UtilitiesAsk whether water, electricity, gas and internet are separately connected.
Renter insuranceConsider contents cover and understand what the landlord's policy excludes.

Protect yourself

  • Never transfer money for a property you cannot verify.
  • Keep the lease, receipts, condition report and inspection photos.
  • Learn the state tenancy authority's rules for repairs, notice and disputes.
  • Do not assume every fee is legal; ask the local consumer or tenancy authority.
