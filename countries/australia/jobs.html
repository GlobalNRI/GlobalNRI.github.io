Jobs and Careers in Australia for NRIs

Finding work in Australia usually requires the right work permission, a local-style application and evidence that your skills match the role. Regulated professions may need registration before you can practise.

Prepare before applying

  • Check your visa work rights and occupation requirements.
  • Confirm whether a skills assessment or professional registration is needed.
  • Use a clear resume focused on achievements and relevant tools.
  • Compare salary, superannuation, leave, location and working hours together.

Build an Australian application

  • Keep your resume concise and tailored to the job description.
  • Write a short cover letter when requested and address the selection criteria.
  • Prepare professional references and explain overseas experience in local terms.
  • Use examples that show safety, teamwork, communication and measurable outcomes.

Finding work

  1. Search Workforce Australia, employer sites and professional associations.
  2. Network through industry events, alumni groups and relevant communities.
  3. Prepare for behavioural interviews using specific situation and result examples.
  4. Check the Fair Work minimum entitlements before accepting an offer.
