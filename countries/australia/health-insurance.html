Health Insurance in Australia for Newcomers

Whether you can use Medicare depends on your visa and circumstances. Some temporary visa holders must maintain Overseas Visitors Health Cover, so check your visa conditions before cancelling private insurance.

First steps after arrival

  • Check Medicare eligibility and enrol through Services Australia if eligible.
  • Confirm whether your visa requires Overseas Visitors Health Cover.
  • Choose a general practitioner and ask how the clinic bills patients.
  • Keep policy documents, vaccination records, medicines and emergency contacts together.

Medicare and private cover

  • Medicare: Public healthcare support for eligible people under Australian rules.
  • Private hospital cover: May offer hospital choice and help with private treatment costs.
  • Extras cover: Can contribute toward services such as dental, optical or physiotherapy, subject to limits.
  • OVHC: Health cover designed for many visitors and temporary residents.

Finding care

  1. Use a GP for routine care, referrals and ongoing conditions.
  2. Use Healthdirect for health information and service navigation.
  3. Call 000 for a life-threatening emergency.
  4. Ask about bulk billing, gap fees, waiting periods and annual limits before treatment.
