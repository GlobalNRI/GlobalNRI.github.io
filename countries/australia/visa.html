Australia Visa and Immigration Guide for NRIs

Australia visa rules are occupation-, sponsor- and stream-specific. Check the Department of Home Affairs before relying on a migration agent, old blog post or social-media checklist.

Common pathways

  • Skilled visas: Points-tested or state-nominated options may suit eligible occupations and applicants.
  • Employer-sponsored visas: An approved employer may nominate you for a role that meets the visa requirements.
  • Student visas: Require enrolment and evidence that you meet the relevant student conditions.
  • Partner and family visas: Available only to eligible relationships and sponsors under the applicable rules.

Prepare before applying

  • Check the current occupation list, age, English, skills assessment and points requirements.
  • Collect passports, birth and marriage certificates, police checks and employment evidence.
  • Arrange an approved English test and skills assessment where the stream requires them.
  • Budget for application charges, medicals, translations and professional advice.

After arrival

  1. Confirm your visa conditions in VEVO and record expiry or travel-facility dates.
  2. Apply for a Tax File Number and set up a bank account.
  3. Check Medicare eligibility and arrange private cover if required by your visa.
  4. Keep your address, passport and family details updated with Home Affairs.
