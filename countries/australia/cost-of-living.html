Cost of Living in Australia

Housing and transport often determine the biggest difference between Australian cities. Compare your expected take-home pay with rent, commuting, utilities, food and childcare before choosing a location.

Budget before you move

  • Compare weekly rent, not only monthly salary figures.
  • Ask whether water, electricity, gas or internet is included in a rental.
  • Budget for a bond, advance rent, furniture and transport from the airport.
  • Include Medicare gaps, private insurance, childcare and school costs where relevant.

City comparison checklist

  • Compare suburbs rather than comparing only Sydney, Melbourne or Brisbane averages.
  • Check public transport, school zones, local climate and access to specialist healthcare.
  • Use the Australian Taxation Office calculator to estimate take-home pay.
  • Keep a buffer for annual bills, car repairs, medical costs and return travel to India.
