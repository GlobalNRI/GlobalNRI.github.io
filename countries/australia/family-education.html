Family and Education in Australia

Schooling, childcare and family support are shared across Australian governments. State or territory rules, your address and your visa status can affect enrolment, fees and available services.

Family settlement checklist

  • Bring birth certificates, school reports, vaccination records and visa documents.
  • Check the local public school catchment before signing a rental agreement.
  • Ask about English as an Additional Language support and learning assistance.
  • Apply early for childcare and compare hours, fees, meals and waiting lists.

School and childcare steps

  1. Contact the state or territory education department or local school directly.
  2. Confirm enrolment documents, starting dates, uniforms, transport and fees.
  3. Ask about before- and after-school care if both parents work.
  4. Keep emergency contacts and medical information current with the school.

Choose a family-friendly suburb

  • Check the catchment using the official school finder, not only a property listing.
  • Compare commute, public transport, parks, libraries and healthcare access.
  • Ask about local playgroups, sports, community centres and newcomer services.
  • Review family payments and childcare support through Services Australia.
