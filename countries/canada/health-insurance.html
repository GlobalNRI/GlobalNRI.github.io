Health Insurance in Canada for Newcomers

Canada has publicly funded provincial and territorial healthcare. You normally apply through the province or territory where you live, and the rules differ by location and immigration status.

First steps after arrival

  • Check the eligibility rules for your province or territory.
  • Apply for the provincial health card as soon as you are eligible.
  • Carry proof of status, identity and address to the application appointment.
  • Buy temporary private cover if a waiting period or coverage gap applies to you.

What public coverage usually means

  • Medically necessary hospital and physician services are generally covered for eligible residents.
  • Prescription medicines outside hospital, dental care, routine vision care and many paramedical services may need separate coverage.
  • Some employers provide extended health benefits. Read the limits, deductible and dependent rules carefully.

Finding care

  1. Use a family doctor or nurse practitioner for routine care when available.
  2. Use a walk-in clinic or provincial virtual-care service for non-emergency needs.
  3. Call emergency services for urgent, life-threatening symptoms.
  4. Keep a list of medicines and allergies, including generic names.
