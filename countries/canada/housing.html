Renting a Home in Canada for NRIs

Rental rules, deposits and application checks differ by province and territory. Start with a short-term option if you are unfamiliar with the city, then learn the local tenancy rules before signing a long lease.

Before signing a lease

  • Confirm the landlord, address, rent amount, lease term and included utilities.
  • Ask what documents are accepted when you have no Canadian credit history.
  • Inspect the home and record existing damage with dated photos.
  • Understand the legal rules for deposits, rent increases, repairs and notice periods.

Typical move-in costs

First rent paymentConfirm the due date and payment method.

Tenant insuranceSome landlords require proof before move-in.

Avoid rental scams

  • Do not send money before verifying the property and the landlord.
  • Be cautious when the rent is far below comparable local listings.
  • Use a written lease and keep every message and receipt.
  • Contact the provincial tenancy authority when you need help with a dispute.
