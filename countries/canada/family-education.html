Family and Education in Canada

Education and childcare are managed mainly by provinces and territories. Your best choices depend on your child's age, language needs, school catchment, work schedule and the support available near your home.

Family settlement checklist

  • Bring birth certificates, school records, vaccination history and custody documents.
  • Check the local school board and enrolment deadline before choosing a rental.
  • Ask about newcomer language support and special education services.
  • Compare childcare availability, hours, fees and subsidy eligibility.

School and childcare steps

  1. Find the school board serving your address and ask which documents are required.
  2. Arrange an assessment if your child needs English or French language support.
  3. Apply early for licensed childcare because waiting lists can be long.
  4. Keep records of immunisations and emergency contacts updated.

Choosing a family neighbourhood

  • Check the school catchment separately from the neighbourhood name.
  • Compare commute time, public transport, parks, libraries and healthcare access.
  • Ask the school about bus routes, lunch arrangements and before- or after-school care.
  • Explore provincial child benefits and tax credits using official calculators.
