Cost of Living in Canada

Your biggest costs will usually be housing, transport, food, utilities and taxes. The same salary can feel very different in Toronto, Vancouver, Calgary, Montreal or a smaller city.

Build your monthly budget

  • Start with rent and tenant insurance before choosing a neighbourhood.
  • Separate fixed bills from seasonal heating and electricity costs.
  • Budget for provincial taxes, sales tax and work-related commuting.
  • Keep an emergency fund for deposits, winter clothing, health gaps and moving costs.

Province and city checklist

  • Compare take-home pay after federal and provincial deductions.
  • Check transit access, school catchments and access to South Asian groceries.
  • Ask whether rent includes heat, water, electricity or internet.
  • Use official provincial tax and benefits calculators for your household.
