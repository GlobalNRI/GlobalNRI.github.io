Canada Visa and Immigration Guide for NRIs

Canada immigration depends on your purpose, occupation, education, language score, funds and province. Start with the official eligibility checker before paying an agent or submitting an application.

Common routes

  • Express Entry: A points-based system for eligible economic immigration programs.
  • Provincial Nominee Programs: Provinces and territories nominate candidates for their labour needs.
  • Work permit: An employer-specific or open permit may be available depending on your situation.
  • Study permit: A study route can require a letter of acceptance, proof of funds and other documents.
  • Family sponsorship: Eligible Canadian citizens and permanent residents may sponsor qualifying family members.

Documents to prepare

  • Valid passport and civil-status documents.
  • Education Credential Assessment where required.
  • Approved language test results in English or French.
  • Employment reference letters and proof of work history.
  • Police certificates, medical examination and proof of funds when requested.

After approval

  1. Check the conditions and expiry date on your permit or confirmation.
  2. Arrange housing, a Canadian bank account, a Social Insurance Number and provincial health registration.
  3. Keep copies of every application, approval and travel document.
  4. Follow the conditions of your status and track renewal dates early.
